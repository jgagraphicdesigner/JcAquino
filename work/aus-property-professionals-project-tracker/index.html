The Need

A small team
needed one
shared workspace.

Aus Property Professionals had different people reviewing, approving, designing, and publishing recurring work. A standard project management subscription would add another ongoing tool cost, and most SaaS platforms were heavier than what the team actually needed.

I built a private APP Project Management app for internal use. Everyone can log in from desktop or mobile, choose their workspace, and manage projects across spaces like Email, PDF, Prints, and Facebook Ads.

The result is a custom tool built around the client's exact workflow, not a generic platform they have to keep paying for every month. It is designed as a forever-free internal app for the client team.

What I Built

A focused app

Team Login

Each team member can sign in under their role, so the app feels like a private workspace instead of a public client portal.

Spaces

Work is grouped into spaces including Email, PDF, Prints, and Facebook Ads, keeping different deliverables separated and easier to scan.

Project Board

The board tracks each project by stage, status, type, assignee, priority, and deadline so the team knows what needs attention.

Mobile PWA

The app can be used from desktop or mobile and installed like an app, giving the team access without app-store overhead.

Collaboration

Chat, comments, file library, notifications, and profile tools keep feedback and project context inside the same system.

Client-Owned Tool

The build avoids the usual monthly project-management SaaS bill by giving the client a custom internal tool for their own workflow.

04 ContentApprover reviews message, accuracy, and direction.
05 DesignDesign build turns the approved draft into final creative.
06 FinalFinal approval confirms everything is ready.
