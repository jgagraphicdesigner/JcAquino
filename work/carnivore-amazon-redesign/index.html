Product focus

Formula

04 // Design breakdown

What changed, module by module

  1. 01 Opening hook

    BeforePure daily electrolytes
    The original opens with a jar, serving count, and a row of broad product claims.
    AfterZero sugar, carbs, and calories
    The final opener puts one clear message above the jar and sachets, then groups supporting dietary callouts below.

    Design intent: Establish the product and its positioning before introducing the longer explanation.

  2. 02 Customer proof

    BeforeNo dedicated customer panel
    The original relies on product renders and brand copy, without a separate customer-photo section.
    AfterCustomer photos and audience context
    The final layout introduces customer photos directly after the opener, with the supplied brand's community-size headline.

    Design intent: Bring customer context into the reading order before the comparison and detailed FAQs.

  3. 03 Product benefits

    BeforeSeparate mineral benefit cards
    The original explains sodium, potassium, and magnesium in separate cards.
    AfterA broader benefit overview
    The final layout combines a mixing photo, a short benefits list, and six nutrient icons in one module.

    Design intent: Connect product features to a visible use case without scattering the information across the page.

  4. 04 Product comparison

    BeforeNo side-by-side chart
    The original describes the product without placing it next to another option.
    AfterA shared comparison table
    The final chart aligns sodium, potassium, sugar, sweeteners, taste, mixing, dyes, and manufacturing callouts across the same rows.

    Design intent: Give the shopper a consistent visual structure for comparing the supplied product information.

  5. 05 FAQs

    BeforeBenefits rather than direct answers
    The original's use-case and dietary callouts do not form a dedicated question-and-answer section.
    AfterThree focused FAQ panels
    The final design separates formula questions, mixing and daily use, and tub-versus-sachet product details into three panels.

    Design intent: Group related questions so the reader can find an answer without searching through benefit copy.

  6. 06 Formula & product facts

    BeforeNo separate facts module
    The original A+ layout uses ingredient-benefit copy rather than a dedicated Supplement Facts presentation.
    AfterProduct facts beside the pack
    The final module shows the supplied Supplement Facts and manufacturing graphics next to the product and per-serving figures.

    Design intent: Give detailed product information its own place in the sequence, separate from promotional headlines.

  7. 07 Daily routine

    BeforeBroad use-case callouts
    The original mentions settings such as home, work, travel, and training.
    AfterA closing routine module
    The final module uses a lifestyle photo and a three-part daily-use timeline as the closing visual.

    Design intent: End the layout with a clear routine narrative. The timeline is part of the supplied creative, not measured portfolio results.

This comparison reviews the design and layout, not measured conversion results. Product, customer, and manufacturing claims shown in the artwork are supplied brand content.
