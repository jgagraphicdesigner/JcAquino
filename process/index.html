How long does a typical project take?
Ad creative projects typically take 24–48 hours, depending on the number of designs and the complexity of the brief. Larger batches may take longer. GHL setups usually take 1–3 weeks, and app development takes 3–8 weeks, depending on scope. I'll confirm your project timeline in the proposal before work begins.
Do you work with international clients?
Yes most of your clients are based in Australia, the US, and the UK. I work fully remote and am available for calls in most time zones.
Can a project expand later?
Absolutely. Many clients start with ad creative and later add GHL funnels or app development. I'm set up to grow with your business the more context I have, the better the work gets.
